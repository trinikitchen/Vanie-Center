A MOTHER’S VISION • EST. 2025

Hello, I’m Vandana Harbance.

30-year-old Hunter College graduate, former three-job hustler, and proud stay-at-home mom to Benjamin (Jan 2023) and Avani (Nov 2023).

I built my first business at 12 — buying wholesale costume jewelry and selling it to my classmates. That same entrepreneurial spirit never left me. After years of working and studying while pregnant, I chose to pour my energy into motherhood… and now I’m ready to bring it full circle.

Giggle & Gathering Club is the space I always wished existed when I became a mom: an elegant, intimate venue where parents and children truly connect through play, creativity, and learning.
